Retail Packaging Solutions

Retail packaging is structured to support consumer-ready presentation while maintaining product protection and regulatory alignment across domestic and international markets.

These packaging systems demonstrate our operational capability in retail-ready formats and can be fully adapted for customized private label programs tailored to your brand specifications.

Packaging Capabilities:

  • Shelf-Ready Formats: Stand-up pouch systems engineered for max product stability

02. INDUSTRIAL EXPORT SOLUTIONS

Bulk Packaging

Bulk packaging supports industrial handling, food manufacturing, and international export transport.

Capabilities:

  • Export-Grade Formats: 20kg to 25kg food-grade poly bags inside 5-ply corrugated master cartons.
  • Customized Weight Specifications: Tailored batch weight fills according to buyer requirements.
  • Moisture-Protective Materials: High-density poly-liners and nitrogen flushing to prevent oxidation.
  • Container-Optimized Packing: Palletized container loading engineered to maximize cargo volume.
  • Shipment Labeling & Identification: Customs-compliant outer carton markings, barcodes, and lot coding.
