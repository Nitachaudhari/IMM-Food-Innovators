02. INDUSTRIAL EXPORT PACKAGING

Industrial Bulk & Export Packaging Solutions

IMM Food Innovators provides robust, cargo-safe bulk export packaging engineered specifically for international sea freight, cross-country logistics, and industrial food manufacturing plants.

Key Bulk Packaging Capabilities:

  • 25kg & 50kg Multi-Layer HDPE Bag Systems: 25kg & 50kg multi-layer food-grade HDPE bags with moisture-barrier liner, Nitrogen flushing option, and heavy-duty outer poly-woven protection.
  • Bag-in-Box Master Cartons: 20 kg to 25 kg food-grade inner poly bags or heat-sealed aluminum foil bags encased inside heavy-duty 5-ply corrugated master cartons.
  • Multi-Layer Moisture Lock Protection: Triple-barrier food-grade inner liners with vacuum sealing or nitrogen flushing to prevent oxidation and hygroscopic caking during ocean sea transit.
