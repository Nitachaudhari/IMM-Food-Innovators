02. INDUSTRIAL EXPORT PACKAGING

Industrial Bulk & Export Packaging Solutions

IMM Food Innovators provides robust, cargo-safe bulk export packaging engineered specifically for international sea freight, cross-country logistics, and industrial food manufacturing plants. Our packaging systems lock out moisture, light, and ambient contamination to guarantee extended shelf-life and pristine product quality upon global arrival.

Key Bulk Packaging Capabilities:

Standard Bag-in-Box Formats
20 kg to 25 kg food-grade inner poly bags or heat-sealed aluminum foil bags encased inside heavy-duty 5-ply corrugated master cartons.
Multi-Layer Moisture Lock Protection
Triple-barrier food-grade inner liners with vacuum sealing or nitrogen flushing to prevent oxidation and hygroscopic caking.
Tailored Weight & Batch Specifications
Customized net weight fills (10 kg, 15 kg, 20 kg, 25 kg) formatted to meet buyer recipe batching or commercial distribution preferences.
Palletization & Container Optimization
Fumigated wooden or plastic palletizing with stretch wrapping, edge protectors, and optimized container stuffing for maximum FCL/LCL cargo payload.
Customs & Export Regulatory Labeling
Customs-compliant outer carton printing featuring batch numbers, production timestamps, gross/net weight specs, barcode tags, and FSSAI/ISO marks.
