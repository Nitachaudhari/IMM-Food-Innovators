PILLAR 02 • THERMAL PRESERVATION SCIENCE

Low-Temperature Air Dehydration Technology (<45°C - 55°C)

Preserving heat-sensitive bioactives, natural color, original aroma, and dietary fiber without adding synthetic carrier agents or Maltodextrin.

Conventional drying methods like spray-drying subject raw food slurries to extreme thermal shock (160°C to 200°C), degrading heat-sensitive nutrients and requiring up to 30%–50% added Maltodextrin starch fillers. Open solar drying exposes food to dust, humidity variations, and microbial contamination.

IMM Food Innovators LLP utilizes precision low-temperature hot-air dehydration (<45°C - 55°C) inside clean stainless-steel drying chambers. This gentle thermal process gently extracts moisture while leaving cellular structures and natural bioactives 100% intact.

100% Pure Soluble Solids (0% Carrier Agents)

Zero Maltodextrin, zero dextrose, zero starch additives. Just pure green banana, moringa, or spice solids.

Bioactive & Enzyme Preservation

Retains resistant starch (type-2 RS) in raw green raw banana powder, chlorophyll in moringa, and volatile curcumin in turmeric.

Consistent Low Moisture Standard (<4% to 6%)

Low water activity prevents enzymatic browning and microbial spoilage natively, offering an extended 18–24 month shelf life.

END-TO-END PROCESS

Our 6-Step Farm-to-Factory Processing Flow

How our 3 core pillars seamlessly connect to deliver uncompromised purity from Jalgaon farmland to global consumer packaging.

01

Harvest & Farm Audit

Fresh Cavendish green bananas, moringa leaves, and spices harvested from ~250 contracted farm acres in Jalgaon.

02

Washing & Sorting

Ozone-purified water washing removes surface debris, followed by manual inspection sorting to ensure only pristine produce enters production.

03

Low-Temp Dehydration

Gently dried at controlled temperatures (<45°C - 55°C) to lock in natural flavor, vibrant pigments, active enzymes, and dietary fiber.

04

Hygienic Fine Milling

Stainless steel pulverization and sifting into customized mesh particle sizes (20 mesh to 100+ mesh fine powders) under cleanroom conditions.

05

NABL Lab Clearance

Batch sample testing at NABL accredited laboratory (Shree ATR Lab Jalgaon — TC-14380) for moisture, microbial, and purity verification.

06

Moisture-Barrier Dispatch

Sealed into 25kg/50kg HDPE moisture-barrier bulk export drums or custom private-label retail pouches for worldwide shipping.

What makes your dehydrated raw banana powder superior to spray-dried options?

Our low-temperature hot-air dehydration (<45°C - 55°C) preserves natural dietary fiber, essential vitamins, authentic aroma, and resistant starch without adding synthetic carriers like Maltodextrin. Spray drying uses high heat (160°C+) and requires 30–50% added carrier starches.

Can we request accredited lab certificates of analysis (CoA)?

Yes. Every commercial batch undergoes rigorous QA testing. We provide accredited NABL Certificate of Analysis reports from Shree ATR Lab Jalgaon (TC-14380) covering moisture, mesh size, heavy metals, and microbial parameters upon request.
