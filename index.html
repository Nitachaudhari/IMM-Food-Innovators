WHO WE ARE

About IMM Food Innovators LLP

Leading the way in innovative food processing with cutting-edge dehydration technology and global safety standards.

Farm-Direct Purity with ~100 Acres of Contract Farming

At IMM Food Innovators LLP, quality control begins in the field long before raw materials reach the factory gate. We contract-farm approximately 100 acres of prime agricultural land in Jalgaon, Maharashtra, supplying 60–70% of our raw material requirements internally. The remainder is procured from trusted, long-term regional partner growers under strict agronomic standards.

Located in Yawal, our state-of-the-art facility processes fresh Cavendish bananas, spices, and herbal botanicals using low-temperature dehydration. By controlling the entire chain—from seed and cultivation to low-temp processing and cleanroom packaging—we deliver uncompromised purity, full traceability, and consistent bulk supply for global B2B buyers.

OUR OPERATIONAL WORKFLOW

Production & Export Pipeline

A structured export workflow designed to maintain total quality control and accountability across every step of the dehydrated food supply chain.

1

Contract Farming & Origin Oversight

Internal supply from ~100 acres of contract-farmed land (60–70% self-sourced), supplemented by vetted regional partner growers under strict quality protocols.

2

Washing & Sorting

Hygienic sorting, washing, peeling, and mechanical slicing inside food-grade stainless steel preparation areas.

3

Low-Temp Dehydration

Moisture extraction under controlled low-temperature airflow curves inside Suntech dehydration chambers.

4

Fine Pulverization & Grinding

Grinding raw dried slices or herbs into fine powders with controlled mesh size and uniform color distribution.

5

Lab Testing & Quality Check

Analytical testing for moisture content, microbial limits, heavy metals, and pesticide residue verification.

6

Packaging & Logistics

Vacuum sealing, nitrogen flushing, private label packaging, and export dispatch to domestic and global ports.
