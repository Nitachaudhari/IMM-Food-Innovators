Common B2B Inquiries

Clear answers on processing technology, quality standards, MOQs, custom OEM packaging, and global export logistics.

Unlike spray-drying—which uses high heat and requires carrier agents like maltodextrin (often up to 30–40% additives)—our low-temperature dehydration process gently removes moisture while keeping the natural fruit solids 100% intact. This preserves natural dietary fiber, aroma, natural sweetness, color, and key vitamins without artificial additives or anti-caking chemicals.

Our manufacturing plant operates under strict food safety management systems:

  • FSSAI License: No. 11524021000216
  • Standards: ISO 22000 & HACCP compliant hygiene protocols
  • Quality Testing: Every batch is tested for moisture levels (<4%–6%), particle mesh size (60 to 120 mesh), microbial limits (TPC, Yeast & Mold), and physical quality standards.

We provide flexible MOQs tailored to your business scale:

  • Sample Kits: 500g to 2 kg for laboratory or pilot recipe testing.
  • Standard Orders: Starting from 50 kg to 100 kg, dispatching in 3–5 business days.
