# IMM Food Innovators LLP — Standing Brand Governance Rules

## Permanent Brand & Editorial Governance

1. **Rule 1 — Company Age**: IMM Food Innovators LLP was established in **2025**. Never write 'years of legacy,' 'decades of experience,' or imply a long corporate history.
2. **Rule 2 — Official Certification Badges**: Use only IMM's authentic certificate scans in `images/certifications/`. Never use generic stock clipart for FSSAI, ISO, or GMP marks.
3. **Rule 3 — Partner Privacy**: Never name partners (e.g., Yogesh) in public website copy, meta descriptions, or mass outreach. All communications are authored under the entity name **IMM Food Innovators LLP**.
4. **Rule 4 — Lab Evidence Discipline**: Every nutrient, bioactive, and technical claim must be backed by an accredited NABL Certificate of Analysis on file (Shree ATR Lab Jalgaon — TC-14380).
