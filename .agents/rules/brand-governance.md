# IMM Food Innovators LLP — Standing Brand Governance Rules

Permanent editorial and operational brand governance standards to observe across all website pages, blogs, metadata, media assets, and collateral.

## Rule 1 — Company Age & History
- **Establishment Date**: IMM Food Innovators LLP was established in **2025**.
- **Prohibited Phrases**: Never write "years of legacy," "decades of experience," "years of excellence," or imply a long corporate history.
- **Approved Framing**: Highlight modern low-temperature dehydration processing technology, ~250 acres contract farming, Jalgaon agricultural origin, and ISO 9001:2015 / ISO 22000:2018 certified facilities.

## Rule 2 — Official Certification Badges & Scans
- **Authentic Scans**: Use only authentic certificate scans and verified seals located in `images/certifications/` and `images/certifications/badges/`.
- **No Generic Clipart**: Never use generic stock clipart for FSSAI, ISO 9001, ISO 22000, GMP, or Udyam MSME marks.

## Rule 3 — Partner Privacy & Entity Communications
- **Entity Representation**: All communications, public website copy, blog posts, meta tags, press releases, and outreach must be authored solely under the entity name **IMM Food Innovators LLP**.
- **Partner Privacy**: Never mention partner names (e.g., Yogesh or individual partner names) in public website copy, metadata, or external marketing collateral.

## Rule 4 — Lab Evidence Discipline
- **NABL Accreditation**: Every nutritional, bioactive, chemical, and micro-particle specification claim must be backed by an accredited NABL Certificate of Analysis on file.
- **Accredited Laboratory Reference**: Independent NABL testing is conducted via **Shree ATR Lab Jalgaon (Certificate No. TC-14380)**.
